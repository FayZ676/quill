# Quill Privacy Policy

_Effective October 21, 2026_

Quill doesn't collect, store or share any of your data.

**Your writing.** Your documents are plain files in a folder you choose: on your device, or in iCloud Drive if you pick a folder there. Quill reads and writes those files and nothing else. iCloud Drive syncing is handled by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); Quill never sees your documents on any server.

**Your settings.** Preferences such as font, text size and focus mode are saved on your device only.

**No tracking.** Quill has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third-party code. It makes no network requests of its own.

**The terminal.** Quill's terminal runs your own shell, and the programs you start in it (an AI tool, say) do whatever they do under their own terms. Quill doesn't see or send what passes through it.

**Children.** Quill collects no information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

**Questions?** Open an issue at [github.com/FayZ676/quill/issues](https://github.com/FayZ676/quill/issues).
